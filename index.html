StudyPomodoro – Free Online Pomodoro Study Timer & Lofi Music

StudyPomodoro is a free, modern focus timer application designed to help students, developers, and knowledge workers enter deep flow states. Built around the proven Pomodoro Technique, StudyPomodoro alternates 25-minute high-focus work intervals with 5-minute restorative breaks, accompanied by soothing Lo-Fi background music and atmospheric themes.

How the Pomodoro Technique Boosts Productivity

Created by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s, the Pomodoro Technique uses structured time intervals to eliminate distractions, reduce mental exhaustion, and maintain consistent focus throughout the day. After completing four consecutive 25-minute focus sessions, users take an extended 15-to-30-minute long break to consolidate memory and recharge cognitive energy.

Core Features of StudyPomodoro

  • Customizable Interval Timer: Default 25-minute study sessions, 5-minute short breaks, and 15-minute long breaks, fully adjustable between 1 and 90 minutes.
  • Ambient Lo-Fi Music & Audio Player: Ten built-in curated chillhop tracks plus integrated background YouTube streaming with dedicated volume control.
  • Embedded Task & Note Management: Keep a clear list of study tasks with priority chips, color labels, and due dates right beside the focus clock.
  • Daily Analytics & Discipline Stats: Automatically log completed focus sessions, study minutes, and taken breaks, stored safely on your device.
  • 100% Free & Local-First Privacy: No advertisements, no analytics trackers, and no login required. Your data never leaves your browser.
  • Progressive Web App (PWA): Install StudyPomodoro on Windows, macOS, iPhone, iPad, and Android with complete offline functionality.
